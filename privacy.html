Your Privacy Matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Crossdresser's Desires East Rand may collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website, contact us or become a member.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Crossdresser's Desires East Rand is an 18+ lifestyle and community platform designed for adults who are interested in feminine expression, fashion, confidence, friendship, community and related adult lifestyle experiences.

We understand that privacy and discretion can be particularly important to members of our community. We therefore aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for operating the website and providing our services.

Privacy principle: We will not publicly disclose personal information simply because someone visits our website or becomes a member.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

Account Information

  • Name or chosen display name
  • Email address
  • WhatsApp number or telephone number, where provided
  • Account login information
  • Membership information

Profile Information

  • Profile biography
  • Interests
  • Fashion and lifestyle interests
  • Profile photographs uploaded by the member
  • Other information a member chooses to make visible

Contact Information

If you contact us through the website, email or WhatsApp, we may receive the information contained in your enquiry. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, WhatsApp number, enquiry type and the contents of your message.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage member accounts
  • Provide membership services
  • Display member profiles according to selected visibility settings
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Communicate about membership and community activities
  • Organise events and experiences
  • Improve the website and user experience
  • Protect the platform from abuse, fraud or unauthorised activity
  • Meet applicable legal obligations

We do not intend to use member information for purposes that are materially unrelated to the services described here without appropriate notice or consent where required.

5. Email, WhatsApp & Communication

Our website may provide links that allow visitors to contact us through email or WhatsApp.

When you choose to use those services, the information you send is also processed by the relevant communication provider.

We recommend that you avoid sending passwords, payment credentials, identity documents or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email or WhatsApp unless specifically requested through a secure and appropriate process.

6. Cookies & Analytics

The website may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies to provide basic functionality and improve the experience.

We may also use analytics tools to understand general website traffic, such as which pages receive visits and how visitors interact with the website.

Analytics information may be aggregated or otherwise processed by third-party service providers in accordance with their own policies.

You can generally control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Security measures may include access controls, authentication, secure hosting practices, restricted administrative access and technical monitoring.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Members should also protect their passwords, devices and account information.

Your responsibility: Never share your member password with another person. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us as soon as possible.

8. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell member personal information.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide services, such as hosting, authentication, payment processing, email or technical services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process or a lawful request from an authorised authority.

We may also take appropriate action where disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect users, the community, the website or another person's safety.

9. Data Retention

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate operational needs or where retention is required by law.

When a member closes an account, certain information may need to be retained for legitimate reasons, including legal, security, accounting or fraud-prevention requirements.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to the personal information we hold about you.

These may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information we hold about you
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information
  • Asking us to delete information where there is no legitimate reason for us to retain it
  • Asking about how your information is being used
  • Raising a privacy concern or complaint

Requests may be subject to reasonable verification requirements to ensure that personal information is not disclosed to the wrong person.

11. Adults Only

Crossdresser's Desires is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and older.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under the age of 18.

18+ only: Do not create an account or participate in the member community if you are under 18.

12. Privacy Within The Community

Privacy is not only a technical responsibility. It is also a community responsibility.

Members are expected to respect the privacy of other members and should never copy, distribute, publish or share another member's private information, photographs or communications without appropriate permission.

Attempts to expose, harass, impersonate, threaten or deliberately identify another member may result in account restrictions or removal from the community.

13. Changes To This Policy

As the website, membership platform and services develop, this Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

The latest version will be published on this page. Where appropriate, we may also provide members with additional notice of material changes.

14. Contact Us

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, your personal information or privacy within the community, please contact us.

Email: crossdress.desires@outlook.com

We will make reasonable efforts to respond to legitimate privacy enquiries and requests.
