Crossdresser's Desires is being built with a long-term vision: a private, welcoming community and eventually a real destination where adults can express themselves, connect with others and feel completely comfortable being themselves.

The Beginning

Every Great Community Starts With A Vision.

Crossdresser's Desires is more than a website. The goal is to create a genuine community built around privacy, respect, confidence, friendship and personal expression.

Our long-term dream is The Desires House — a dedicated private venue where members can meet, relax, learn, create, socialise and enjoy experiences in a welcoming environment.

Donations are completely separate from membership and are intended to help us develop that future vision.

The Desires House

What We Hope To Create

The physical venue is a future goal. We want to grow the community first and build the venue responsibly when the time is right.

Transformation

Private makeup, dressing and styling spaces where members can explore fashion and personal expression.

Community

Comfortable lounges and social spaces where members can meet people, make friends and share experiences.

Creativity

Photography, fashion and creative spaces designed to encourage confidence and self-expression.

Well-Being

A supportive environment with access to appropriately qualified professionals and referrals for members seeking personal or gender-related support.

Transparency Matters

Built Step By Step.

Crossdresser's Desires is currently an online community concept. The Desires House does not exist yet.

Contributions towards the future vision will be used to support legitimate development costs associated with building the community and, when feasible, establishing a future venue.

We believe members and supporters should always understand what they are supporting and where the project currently stands.
