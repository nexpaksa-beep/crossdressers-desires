The Desires Standard

Our community works best when everyone understands the same basic expectations: adults only, mutual respect, clear consent, personal privacy and responsible behaviour.

1. Our Community

Crossdresser's Desires is designed for adults who are interested in crossdressing, feminine expression, fashion, beauty, confidence, friendship, photography, social activities and related lifestyle interests.

Members may have very different backgrounds, identities, styles, experiences and reasons for being part of the community. Those differences should be treated with dignity.

Everyone deserves a comfortable space to be themselves.

You do not need to look, dress or express yourself in a particular way to belong here.

2. 18+ Only

Crossdresser's Desires is an adults-only community.

Members must be at least 18 years old to create an account, participate in member activities or access members-only areas.

Adults Only

Do not create an account on behalf of a person under 18. Do not upload, request, share or distribute content involving anyone under 18.

3. Respect For Others

Treat other members as people, not as objects or assumptions.

  • Speak to others respectfully.
  • Respect different styles, identities and experiences.
  • Do not bully, threaten, intimidate or harass another member.
  • Do not deliberately humiliate another person.
  • Respect someone's decision when they do not want to communicate.
  • Do not pressure another member into meeting, sharing information or participating in an activity.

4. Consent

Consent is fundamental to our community.

Consent should be freely given, informed and respected. A person can change their mind at any time.

No means no.

If someone does not want to communicate, meet, participate, share a photograph or continue an interaction, respect that decision.

Consent must never be assumed simply because someone has created a profile, attended an event or communicated with another member.

5. Privacy & Discretion

Privacy is one of the foundations of Crossdresser's Desires.

  • Do not share another member's private information without permission.
  • Do not publish screenshots of private conversations without appropriate permission.
  • Do not reveal another member's identity, workplace, address or personal circumstances.
  • Do not photograph or record another member at an event without their permission.
  • Respect members who choose to remain private or use a preferred name.

Members should also take reasonable steps to protect their own personal information when interacting online or meeting people in person.

6. Member Profiles

Profiles are intended to help members introduce themselves and discover shared interests.

Please provide information that is truthful and appropriate for a private adult community.

  • Do not impersonate another person.
  • Do not create fake profiles to deceive or manipulate members.
  • Do not upload another person's photographs without permission.
  • Do not use someone else's identity or personal information.
  • Keep profile information respectful and suitable for the community.

7. Communication & Messaging

Members should use messaging and communication features responsibly.

  • Be polite when starting a conversation.
  • Respect unanswered messages.
  • Do not repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop.
  • Do not threaten or intimidate another member.
  • Do not use the platform for fraud, scams or deceptive activities.
  • Do not distribute another member's private conversations without appropriate permission.

8. Member Content

Members may eventually be able to share photographs, profile information, stories and other community content.

Content shared through the platform should respect the privacy, dignity and rights of everyone involved.

  • Only share content that you have the right to share.
  • Do not upload another person's private material without permission.
  • Do not use content to threaten, embarrass or blackmail another person.
  • Do not upload illegal content.
  • Do not upload content involving minors.
Think Before You Share

Once something has been copied, saved or shared elsewhere, it may be difficult to control where it goes. Protect yourself and respect the privacy of others.

9. Events & Experiences

Future Crossdresser's Desires events and experiences will operate around the same principles as the online community.

  • Members must follow venue rules and event instructions.
  • Respect other attendees and event staff.
  • Ask before photographing or recording other people.
  • Respect personal boundaries.
  • Do not attend while behaving in a way that creates a safety risk to others.
  • Follow any additional rules communicated for a specific event.

Specific event terms, venue requirements and safety rules may apply to individual events.

10. Behaviour That Is Not Permitted

The following types of behaviour are not compatible with the Crossdresser's Desires community:

  • Harassment, bullying or threats.
  • Hate speech or discriminatory abuse.
  • Impersonation or deliberate deception.
  • Doxxing or exposing private information.
  • Blackmail, extortion or intimidation.
  • Fraud, scams or financial deception.
  • Unauthorised recording or distribution of private material.
  • Sharing content involving minors.
  • Illegal activity.
  • Deliberately bypassing security, privacy or access controls.
  • Creating accounts for the purpose of abusing or disrupting the community.

11. Reporting A Concern

If you believe another member has violated these guidelines, please report the concern rather than attempting to resolve serious situations through confrontation.

When reporting a concern, provide as much relevant information as you can, including the nature of the issue and any available evidence.

Need To Speak With Us?

For membership, community or safety-related concerns, contact Crossdresser's Desires directly.

Email Us WhatsApp

12. Moderation & Community Safety

As the community develops, appropriate moderation processes may be introduced to help maintain a safe, respectful and private environment.

Depending on the circumstances, moderation may include reviewing a reported account or piece of content, requesting clarification, restricting access to features, removing content or suspending an account.

Serious or unlawful conduct may be referred to the appropriate authorities where required or appropriate.

Moderation is about protecting the community.

The goal is to maintain an environment where adults can participate without harassment, intimidation or unnecessary exposure of their private lives.

13. Look After Yourself Too

Community guidelines can help create a respectful environment, but members should also make sensible decisions about their own safety and privacy.

  • Avoid sharing sensitive personal information too quickly.
  • Be careful when moving conversations to external platforms.
  • Consider meeting new people in appropriate public or organised settings.
  • Tell someone you trust if you are meeting someone new.
  • Trust your instincts if an interaction makes you uncomfortable.

14. The Desires Standard

The simplest way to understand these guidelines is:

Be yourself.

Express yourself with confidence and authenticity.

Respect others.

Everyone has their own story, boundaries and journey.

Protect privacy.

Treat personal information and private moments with care.

Ask for consent.

Never assume permission. Respect the answer you receive.

Keep the community safe.

Speak up about serious concerns and help create an environment where people can participate comfortably.
