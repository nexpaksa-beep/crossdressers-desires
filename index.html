More than a night out

A place to finally feel comfortable being yourself.

For many crossdressers, finding somewhere private, welcoming and judgement-free can be difficult.

Crossdresser's Desires is being created to provide a community where adults can explore their feminine expression, meet like-minded people and enjoy professionally organised experiences in a discreet environment.

Our long-term vision is a dedicated private destination where members can arrive, transform, socialise, stay overnight and enjoy an entire weekend without having to hide who they want to be.

"Sometimes you don't need to become someone else. You simply need somewhere safe enough to become yourself." Crossdresser's Desires East Rand
