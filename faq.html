General Questions

Crossdresser's Desires is a private 18+ community focused on feminine expression, confidence, fashion, friendship, social connection and shared experiences.

The community is being developed with privacy, discretion, consent and respectful adult interaction at its centre.

No. People can be at very different stages of their journey.

Some members may have years of experience, while others may only recently have started exploring feminine expression.

What matters is that members are adults and treat one another with respect.

The community is focused on the East Rand, Johannesburg and the wider Gauteng area.

The online community can connect adults regardless of exactly where they live.

Membership

Membership is intended for adults aged 18 and older who are interested in crossdressing, feminine expression, fashion, confidence, friendship or community.

All members must follow the community guidelines and terms of membership.

The planned founding membership is R199 per month.

Membership pricing, inclusions and future benefits may change as the community develops. Any material changes will be communicated to members.

Planned member benefits include a member profile, access to the private community, member experiences, social opportunities and future member events.

Additional benefits may be added as the community grows.

Membership cancellation will be subject to the membership terms and the payment system used at the time.

Members should review the applicable cancellation and billing information before completing a subscription.

Privacy & Discretion

Public-facing member profiles may use a chosen name or display name rather than a legal name.

However, any information required for account verification, payment, safety or legal compliance must be provided accurately where required.

The community is designed around privacy and discretion, but no online service can guarantee that information will never be seen, copied or disclosed by another person.

Members should only share information they are comfortable sharing and should respect the privacy of others.

Profile visibility and privacy controls are intended to allow members to decide what information they share with the community.

The final controls will depend on the member platform and functionality implemented.

Profiles & Community

Profile content must remain within the community guidelines and must respect other members.

Planned member functionality may allow members to upload photographs to their profiles.

Uploaded content must comply with the community rules, privacy requirements and applicable law.

Events & Experiences

Planned activities may include welcome evenings, fashion nights, makeup and transformation sessions, photography evenings, social gatherings and future weekend retreats.

Event access may differ depending on the event.

Some activities may be member-only, while selected introductory or community activities may have different attendance requirements.

Privacy and discretion are core principles of the community.

Specific event arrangements, photography rules, guest policies and venue requirements will be communicated for each event.

Well-Being Support

The future Well-Being Centre is envisioned as a supportive space where adults can access appropriate professional wellbeing or counselling services related to identity, confidence and personal development.

It is intended to provide support, not to pressure anyone toward a particular identity, outcome or personal decision.

The Well-Being Centre is part of the future vision for The Desires House and should not be assumed to be an operating service unless specifically announced.
